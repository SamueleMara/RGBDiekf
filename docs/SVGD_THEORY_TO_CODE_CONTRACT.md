## 1. Selected model (what is exact, what is proposed, what is missing)

- **Joint target (PDF (29), E1)**: p(X, m, Z, V, B | Q_0:T) ∝ p(Q | X, V, B) p(X | m, Z) p(V, B, m, Z). Only the factors marked EXACT_MODEL below are implemented as the theory states them; p(V | X, C), B_t, p(m, Z), the moving-motion prior, Γ_t and Ω_model are **NOT_DEFINED_IN_THEORY**.
- **Observation support**: every mask-positive valid-depth pixel of every camera (`scene/pointcloud_sequence.py`), world XYZ [m], encoded RGB in [0,1] as loaded; no background, no subsampling of inference points, frames or cameras; source triple (frame id, camera, pixel) unique.
- **Sample weighting**: w_j = 1/M_t (PDF (8)); predicted α_i = 1/|V_t| (PDF (18)) on a V_t that the theory does not define.
- **Shared uncertainty**: the frame-0 back-projection covariance Σ_i^0 enters ONCE, in P_i0 of the temporal prior (C4); the rigidity covariance counts the shared reference once (rank-1 term c 11ᵀ).
- **Proposal vs likelihood**: the KDE score g_t (E03) and the Stein field Φ (E07) are **proposals only**; the single statistical observation is the feature likelihood E_F,t (E12). The score is never added to the objective; Φ is never assimilated.
- **Whole sequence**: one objective over all frames, E = Σ_t E_F,t(X_t) + E_time(S) (E32, conditional); later frames revise earlier ones through the coupled temporal prior (block-tridiagonal L solved exactly).
- **Probabilities**: no γ_i is published: E25/E26 are BLOCKED_MODEL_UNSPECIFIED (Gate 6). The tiny-model evidence machinery (E27-E31, E33) is exact and tested.
